Privacy Policy

Battlefield Atlas / Savaş Atlası · last updated 30 October 2026

Who we are

Battlefield Atlas is a single-operator documentary project. Its "Battle Engine" software runs only on the operator's own computer and is used to produce and upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channels (Battlefield Atlas in English and Savaş Atlası in Turkish). Contact: y.osmanmelih@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

The app asks Google for permission to upload videos, set thumbnails and captions, and read the channel name and ID of the YouTube channels it publishes to (YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl and youtube.readonly). It is used only with the operator's own channels.

What is stored, and where

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the operator's computer, in a local folder outside the project. They are never sent to any server other than Google's, never shared, never sold and never used for advertising. No personal data of viewers is collected, stored or processed by this app.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token files also removes the app's access.

Türkçe özet

Bu uygulama yalnızca işletmecinin kendi bilgisayarında çalışır ve yalnızca kendi YouTube kanallarına (Savaş Atlası ve Battlefield Atlas) video, küçük resim ve altyazı yüklemek için Google izni ister. Giriş jetonları yalnızca bu bilgisayarda saklanır; hiçbir sunucuya gönderilmez, paylaşılmaz, satılmaz. İzleyicilere ait hiçbir kişisel veri toplanmaz. Erişim her an myaccount.google.com/permissions adresinden geri alınabilir.
